Hi everyone,

I am writing to confirm the details for our first founders' meeting and to share the advance materials we will be discussing. Whether this is your first time hearing the full picture or you have already been deep in the documents, this meeting is designed so that everyone leaves with the same information and the same opportunity to ask questions.

- **Parking:** Free street parking on NE Prescott. There is also a small lot behind the building.
- **Food:** Light food and drinks will be provided.

If you need to attend remotely, a Zoom link will follow by April 25. But if at all possible, please come in person. This meeting works best when people are in the same room.

**Who Will Be There**

Maven (founding organizer), Birch (carpenter), Slate (roofer), Copper (plumber), and myself (Calloway, recruiter and facilitator). Volt (electrician) may also attend as an invited observer, pending confirmation.

**Advance Materials**

Attached to this email are five documents. Please review them before the meeting. You do not need to memorize them. You do need to read them well enough to come with questions.

1. **Pitch Deck** (pitch-deck.md) — Overview of the co-op: what it is, what we do, and why it works. Start here if you want the big picture.

2. **Member Financial Guide** (member-financial-guide.md) — The full financial model: how we buy properties, how rehab costs work, how profits are split, what your labor share and capital share look like in real dollars. This is the document that answers "what do I actually earn?"

3. **Operating Agreement** (operating-agreement.md) — The legal framework: governance structure, voting rights, exit and buyout terms, non-compete, dispute resolution. This is the document that answers "what are the rules and what happens if things go wrong?"

4. **Member Agreement v2** (member-agreement-v2.md) — The individual membership contract template. This is what each member will sign upon joining. It covers your capital contribution, your obligations, and your rights. No one is being asked to sign anything at this meeting.

5. **Meeting Agenda** (founders-meeting-agenda.md) — The full agenda for April 29 so you know what to expect. We will cover the co-op vision, financial model, operating agreement key terms, member agreement overview, and then open the floor for Q&A.

**What to Expect**

This is a working meeting, not a sales pitch. Maven will walk through the vision and financial model. I will cover the operating agreement and member agreement. Then we open the floor. The Q&A session is 30 minutes and there are no off-limits topics. If you have a question about money, liability, governance, scheduling, the non-compete, what happens when people disagree, or anything else — bring it.

**What We Are Not Asking**

No one is being asked to sign anything or commit at this meeting. The purpose is to make sure every person at the table has the same information, has had their questions answered, and has had a chance to meet the other people who may become their co-op partners. Decisions about membership will come later, on your timeline.

**How to Prepare**

- Read the five documents. Mark anything you want to discuss.
- Think about what matters most to you: Is it the financial return? The governance? The people? The type of work? Come ready to say what you need to know in order to make a decision.
- If you have questions before the meeting, call or text me anytime. I am available.

**One More Thing**

This is the first time many of you will be in the same room together. Birch and Copper have worked together before, but Slate and Volt (if attending) will be meeting the group for the first time. That is part of why this meeting exists. The documents tell you how the co-op works. The meeting tells you who the co-op is.

I am looking forward to April 29. This has been months in the making and the cohort we are assembling is strong. See you at Cully.

Best,
