# Skills Matrix — Portland Housing Co-op

**Version:** 1.0  
**Author:** Calloway  
**Date:** 2026-04-08  
**Purpose:** Map required skills for a typical Portland residential rehab against current member coverage. Identify gaps for recruitment targeting.

---

## How to Read This Matrix

**Columns** = Current and pending founding members  
**Rows** = Skills required for a Portland residential rehab project  
**Ratings:**
- 🟢 **5** — Expert (can lead, teach, and troubleshoot)
- 🔵 **4** — Proficient (handles independently, rarely needs guidance)
- 🟡 **3** — Competent (can execute with standard conditions)
- 🟠 **2** — Basic (requires oversight or is a secondary skill)
- ⬜ **—** — Not applicable / insufficient skill

**Priority levels for each skill:**
- **CRITICAL** — Every rehab project needs this. Must be covered by a member.
- **HIGH** — Most projects need this. Should be covered or have a reliable sub.
- **MEDIUM** — Needed on 50–70% of projects. Sub OK if not covered by member.
- **LOW** — Niche skill; subcontract as needed.

**Trade rate weight** = Multiplier applied to logged hours in the labor share calculation.

## Part 4: Recruiting Checklist for Electrician (Priority 1)

The single biggest skills gap is electrical. Here's what we need:

**Required:**
- Active Oregon CCB license (electrical endorsement)
- Oregon Electrical Specialty Code knowledge
- IBEW Local 48 membership (preferred, not required)
- Experience with service panel upgrades (100A → 200A)
- Experience with knob-and-tube identification and remediation
- EPA RRP certification (mandatory for all members)
- GL insurance $500K/$1M minimum

**Ideal profile:**
- 8–12 years residential electrical experience
- Portland-specific experience (old housing stock, K&T, aluminum wiring issues)
- Journeyman or master electrician with own business or LLC
- Collaborative track record with other trades (not a lone wolf)
- Financial capacity for at least Tier C buy-in ($5,000)

**Where to find them:**
1. IBEW Local 48 — SW Portland hall; member board, informal referrals
2. Portland Community College electrical program alumni
3. Oregon CCB licensee search (filter: electrical contractor, residential, Portland zip codes)
4. Referral from Harlan — 20 years of GC relationships means he knows the reliable electricians

**Estimated timeline to recruit:**  
Parallel with Birch / Slate / Copper outreach. Target: founding cohort finalized within 60 days.
