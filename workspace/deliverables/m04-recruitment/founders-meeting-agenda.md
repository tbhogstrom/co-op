## Agenda

### 1. Welcome and Introductions (6:00 - 6:20 / 20 minutes)

  - No one is being asked to sign anything tonight

---

### 2. Co-op Vision and First-Flip Plan (6:20 - 6:40 / 20 minutes)

**Lead:** Maven

- What is the Portland Housing Cooperative — the one-paragraph version
- Why worker-owned: the economics of keeping labor value in-house vs. subcontracting it out
- Target market: pre-1940s Portland residential properties in Lents, Cully, and Parkrose
  - Why these neighborhoods (price point, housing stock, permit environment)
  - What a typical rehab project looks like — scope, timeline, roles
- First-flip concept: acquisition target, estimated scope, projected timeline
  - Conservative scenario: $180K-$220K acquisition, $45K rehab budget, $280K-$320K ARV
  - Target closing: late summer / early fall 2026
- What success looks like after Year 1: 2 completed flips, capital accounts growing, trade coverage proven

**Materials referenced:** pitch-deck.md (distributed in advance)

---

### 3. Financial Model Walkthrough (6:40 - 7:05 / 25 minutes)

**Lead:** Maven (using Ledger's member-financial-guide.md)

- Revenue model: purchase, rehab, sell — gross profit calculation
- How profit is split:
  - 20% to reserve fund (co-op retained earnings)
  - 10% to overhead recovery (operating costs)
  - 30% to capital pool (distributed by capital account proportion)
  - 40% to labor pool (distributed by weighted hours)
- Labor share mechanics:
  - How hours are tracked and weighted (trade multipliers: electrical 1.3x, plumbing 1.3x, roofing 1.2x, carpentry 1.2x, general 1.0x)
  - What "weighted hours" means in real dollars
  - Labor advances: 50% of estimated labor share paid during the project
- Capital share mechanics:
  - How buy-in tiers work (Tier A / B / C)
  - How capital accounts grow over time
  - Projected returns at each tier level
- Walk through the conservative first-flip scenario:
  - $40,750 gross profit
  - Labor pool: $16,300
  - Capital pool: $12,225
  - Individual member estimates based on hours and buy-in tier
- Questions on the financial model (allow extra time here — the money conversation is where people engage or disengage)

**Materials referenced:** member-financial-guide.md (distributed in advance)

---

### 4. Operating Agreement Key Terms (7:05 - 7:25 / 20 minutes)

**Lead:** Calloway (summarizing Statton's work)

- Governance structure:
  - One member, one vote — regardless of capital contribution
  - Majority, supermajority, and unanimous vote thresholds
  - Board of Managers: roles, election, term limits
  - Monthly members' meetings
- Exit and buyout:
  - Voluntary withdrawal: 90 days' notice, capital account returned within 60 days
  - Active project hold provision
  - Installment payout option if co-op is cash-constrained
  - Involuntary exit (expulsion for cause): due process, supermajority vote
- Non-compete:
  - 12 months, Portland metro only, flipping activity only
  - Existing trade businesses explicitly unaffected
  - Waiver available on request
- Dispute resolution:
  - Internal mediation first, then binding arbitration
  - No lawsuits among members unless arbitration fails

**Note:** The amended operating agreement may or may not be finalized by April 29 (Statton target: week of April 22). If the final version is ready, distribute it one week before the meeting. If not, distribute the current draft with a cover note identifying the six pending amendments. Slate has already seen the terms summary and knows amendments are in progress.

**Materials referenced:** operating-agreement.md or amended version (distributed in advance); slate-terms-summary.md available as supplemental reference

### 5. Member Agreement Overview (7:25 - 7:40 / 15 minutes)

**Lead:** Calloway

- What the Member Agreement is: the individual contract each member signs upon joining
- Key provisions:
  - Capital contribution amount and timing
  - Commitment to EPA RRP certification (mandatory for all members)
  - CCB license and insurance maintenance requirements
  - Labor participation expectations
  - Confidentiality of co-op financial and deal information
  - Acknowledgment of operating agreement terms
- What you are signing vs. what you are not signing:
  - You are committing capital and agreeing to the rules
  - You are not giving up your independent business
  - You are not taking on personal liability beyond your capital account
- Timeline: Member Agreements will be prepared individually once candidates are approved by membership vote
