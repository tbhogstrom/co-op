# New Member Onboarding Guide — Portland Housing Cooperative LLC

**Version:** 1.0  
**Author:** Calloway  
**Date:** 2026-04-08  
**Purpose:** Step-by-step process from first contact to active co-op membership. Timelines, checkpoints, and responsible parties for each stage.

---

## Overview: The Onboarding Journey

```
INTERESTED → APPLICATION → VETTING → INTERVIEW → BOARD VOTE → CAPITAL → AGREEMENT → ORIENTATION → ACTIVE MEMBER
```

**Estimated total duration:** 4–8 weeks from first conversation to active membership, depending on candidate responsiveness and board meeting schedule.

---

## Stage 0: Initial Conversation
**Owner:** Calloway  
**Timeline:** Day 1

This is not a formal process step — it's a human conversation. No forms, no commitments, no pressure.

**Purpose:**  
Find out whether this person is genuinely interested in the co-op model or just interested in the money. Both motivations exist; only one produces a good member.

**What Calloway covers:**
- What the co-op is and what it isn't (not a staffing agency, not a passive investment)
- The business model in plain terms: we buy, rehab, sell, split the profit
- The obligations: buy-in capital, active participation, monthly governance
- The timeline: 12–14 months to first distribution — set this expectation early
- What the vetting process looks like so there are no surprises

**What Calloway listens for:**
- Questions about the governance and decision-making (positive signal)
- Questions about the numbers and how distribution is calculated (positive signal)
- "So basically you need me to front you money and wait a year?" (yellow flag — address directly)
- "I don't really do meetings" (red flag — address directly)
- Genuine curiosity about the other founding members and who's in the room (strong positive)

**Outcome:**  
If the candidate wants to move forward, Calloway sends the member application and vetting checklist requirements. No commitment has been made by either party.

## Stage 1: Application
**Owner:** Applicant (Calloway supports)  
**Timeline:** 5–10 business days to complete and return

**Applicant completes:**
- `member-application.md` — full form including skills inventory, licensing, insurance, references, financial capacity, and alignment questions

**Calloway's role:**
- Send the application and any supporting documents (pitch deck if not already provided)
- Be available to answer questions — especially about the EPA RRP requirement
- Log the application in `data/member-registry/` when received

**Checklist before application is considered complete:**

☐ All sections filled out (partial applications are not accepted)  
☐ CCB license number provided  
☐ Insurance carrier and policy number provided  
☐ Surety bond information provided  
☐ EPA RRP certification status confirmed  
☐ Three professional references with contact information  
☐ Buy-in tier selection made (or "need to discuss" noted)  
☐ Attestation signed  

**If EPA RRP is not current:** Document the gap. The applicant must commit to completing certification within 60 days. Add a calendar reminder to follow up. No project participation until certification is confirmed.

## Stage 2: Compliance Verification
**Owner:** Calloway  
**Timeline:** 2–3 business days

Before reviewing the application content, verify the hard requirements. Use the `vetting-checklist.md` for this stage.

**Steps:**

**2.1 CCB License Verification**  
→ Oregon CCB database: [https://www.oregon.gov/ccb](https://www.oregon.gov/ccb)  
→ Search by license number from application  
→ Confirm: active, correct endorsement, no active complaints  
→ Screenshot and save verification to candidate file  

**2.2 Insurance Verification**  
→ Request ACORD certificate of insurance directly from candidate's carrier  
→ Confirm: $500K/$1M GL minimum, policy not expired  
→ Save certificate to candidate file  

**2.3 Surety Bond Verification**  
→ Confirm bond is active and meets CCB minimums  
→ Note expiration date  

**2.4 EPA RRP Verification**  
→ Search EPA certified renovator database  
→ Or request certificate directly from applicant  
→ Note expiration (5-year renewal cycle)  

**2.5 Specialty Trade License**  
→ Plumbers: Oregon BCD license lookup  
→ Electricians: Oregon BCD license lookup  
→ HVAC: Oregon BCD limited energy or mechanical license  

**If any item fails:** Stop. Contact the candidate. Explain the specific deficiency. If it can be remedied (e.g., upcoming insurance renewal, pending EPA RRP course enrollment), set a clear deadline and hold the file. If not remedied, close the file and document the reason.

**If all items pass:** Proceed to scored assessment. Notify the candidate that compliance verification is complete.

## Stage 3: Scored Assessment
**Owner:** Calloway  
**Timeline:** 3–5 business days (includes reference calls)

Using the scoring rubric in `vetting-criteria.md`, Calloway assesses the application across five dimensions (100-point total):

- Technical skills (35 pts)
- Licensing & credentials quality (15 pts)
- Financial capacity (15 pts)
- Culture fit & co-op alignment (25 pts)
- References (10 pts)

**Reference calls (required):**  
All three references must be contacted by phone. Email is not accepted. Log: date, time, duration, key points, any concerns raised.

**Scoring threshold to advance:**  
80+ points: Advance to interview (with notes on areas to probe)  
70–79: Conditional advance (discuss with Maven before scheduling interview)  
Below 70: Do not advance without Maven approval

**Calloway documents the score and the reasoning.** This is not just a number — it's a record of why the candidate is recommended. The file goes to Maven.

---

## Stage 4: Interview
**Owner:** Calloway + Maven (required); one additional founding member (if available)  
**Timeline:** 60–90 minutes; scheduled within 5 business days of Stage 3 completion

**Format:** Conversational, not interrogatory. We review the application together. We ask them to tell us about their work. We let them tell us what concerns them.

**Interview logistics:**
- In person, preferred (we're building a physical relationship with people we'll work job sites with)
- Video call acceptable if in-person scheduling is difficult
- Calloway leads; Maven focuses on co-op alignment and governance questions
- One note-taker (rotate between Calloway and Maven)

**Required discussion topics:**
1. Their best work story — what are they proudest of?
2. A project that went wrong — what happened, how they responded
3. How they handle disagreement with a client or GC
4. Their understanding of the co-op model — any questions or concerns?
5. The financial picture — walk through the profit-split formula and their estimated return
6. The EPA RRP requirement — confirm they understand and are compliant (or timeline to comply)
7. The buy-in — confirm they have reviewed the tier options and have a preference
8. Availability — will they be available for the first rehab project?

**After the interview:**  
Calloway and Maven debrief immediately (15 minutes, same day). Document the outcome. One of three results:

- **Recommend** — Advance to board vote
- **Recommend with conditions** — (e.g., EPA RRP pending, specific concern to revisit)
- **Do not recommend** — Document clearly and respectfully decline

**Declining a candidate:**  
Calloway calls. Not an email. Honest, brief, respectful. "We went another direction with the founding cohort" or "The skills match isn't quite right for our current needs." Never make it personal. Leave the door open for future conversations if appropriate.

## Stage 5: Board Vote
**Owner:** Maven (facilitates)  
**Timeline:** Next scheduled member meeting, or special call within 2 weeks

**Quorum:** All current members must be present or send a proxy.

**Process:**
1. Calloway presents the candidate — summary of background, score, interview notes, recommendation
2. Any member may ask questions or raise concerns
3. Members vote: Yes / No / Abstain
4. **2/3 supermajority required** for founding member admission

**If approved:** Maven notifies the candidate within 24 hours. Calloway sends onboarding documents.

**If declined:** Maven and Calloway align on how to communicate. Calloway calls the candidate.

---

## Stage 6: Capital Contribution
**Owner:** Ledger  
**Timeline:** Within 10 business days of board approval

**Steps:**
1. Ledger sends wire transfer or check instructions for the buy-in amount
2. Candidate confirms their tier selection (Tier A/B/C) — if not already confirmed
3. Capital received and deposited to the co-op operating account
4. Ledger creates the member capital account, credited with buy-in amount
5. Ledger confirms receipt to Maven and Calloway

**No membership is finalized until capital is received.** This is not punitive — it's practical. The co-op needs cash in the bank before the property search begins. Members who experience genuine hardship between approval and contribution should contact Maven immediately.

---

## Stage 7: Member Agreement Signing
**Owner:** Statton (prepares agreement); Member (signs)  
**Timeline:** Concurrent with or immediately after capital contribution

**What the Member Agreement covers:**
- Member's name, trade, and buy-in tier
- Capital account terms (equity, not a loan)
- Profit-split formula reference (per Operating Agreement)
- Labor advance terms (up to 50% of estimated labor share, deducted at distribution)
- Independent contractor-member status (not employee)
- Licensing and insurance obligations (must maintain throughout membership)
- EPA RRP certification obligation
- Exit / withdrawal terms (90-day notice, no withdrawal during active project)
- Governance rights (voting, attendance expectations)
- Confidentiality provisions

**Signing:** In person if possible. Digital signature acceptable via DocuSign or equivalent. Both Calloway (as witness) and Maven (as authorized officer) sign on behalf of the co-op.

**After signing:** Statton retains original. Member receives a copy. Copy goes into the member file.

## Stage 8: New Member Orientation
**Owner:** Calloway  
**Timeline:** Within 2 weeks of Member Agreement signing

**Format:** 2–3 hours in person. The whole founding cohort should attend if possible — this is how the team starts to feel like a team.

**Agenda:**

**Block 1 — The Co-op (60 min)**
- Co-op history and founding decisions (Maven presents)
- Operating Agreement walkthrough — governance, voting, profit split, exit terms (Statton presents or summarizes)
- Financial model walkthrough — how the numbers work, what a distribution looks like (Ledger presents)

**Block 2 — The Work (60 min)**
- Target neighborhoods: Lents, Cully, Parkrose — Reeves walks through the market
- How we source, evaluate, and decide on properties
- Harlan's rehab methodology — how scopes are built, how trades are sequenced
- The labor tracking system — how to log hours, what counts, what doesn't

**Block 3 — Systems & Tools (30 min)**
- Communication: how we communicate between members (platform TBD)
- Shared documents: where to find the current project board, budget reports, meeting notes
- The member registry: your profile, your license expiration dates, your insurance renewal dates
- First meeting date: confirm the standing monthly meeting cadence

**Block 4 — Q&A and Introductions (30 min)**
- Open floor for any questions not covered
- If new member hasn't met all founding members yet: introductions
- The "what I'm hoping for from this co-op" go-around — everyone shares, briefly

**What the new member leaves with:**
☐ Copy of their signed Member Agreement  
☐ Copy of the Operating Agreement  
☐ Access to the co-op's shared drive / project board  
☐ Contact information for all founding members  
☐ Date of next member meeting  
☐ Understanding of the labor hour logging system  
☐ Clarity on their first co-op role / project assignment (if applicable)

## Stage 9: Active Member Status
**Owner:** Member  
**Timeline:** Immediately after orientation

**The member is now a co-owner of the Portland Housing Cooperative LLC.**

**Ongoing obligations (to maintain active status):**
- Keep CCB license current — Calloway will send reminder 90 days before expiration
- Keep GL insurance current — provide updated certificate to Calloway at each renewal
- Keep EPA RRP certification current — 5-year renewal cycle; Calloway tracks
- Attend monthly member meetings (minimum; excused absences with advance notice)
- Log all co-op project hours in the shared tracking system by end of each week
- Notify Maven or Calloway of any CCB complaints, license issues, or insurance lapses immediately

**Annual check-in:**  
Once per year, Calloway runs a compliance re-verification for all members (CCB, insurance, bond, RRP). This is a condition of continued good standing.
