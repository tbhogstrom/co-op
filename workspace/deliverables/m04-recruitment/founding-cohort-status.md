## 5. Risk Factors

### Risk 1: Candidate Drops Out During Vetting

**Likelihood:** Medium (especially Slate, who is the most cautious)  
**Impact:** Delays full cohort by 4-6 weeks while replacement is sourced  
**Mitigation:**
- Maintain warm relationships with all three candidates throughout vetting
- Get Slate in front of the other candidates early — interpersonal chemistry is the closer
- If Birch or Copper drops (lower probability), activate Harlan's network immediately for trade replacement
- Keep a secondary carpenter and plumber contact warm in the background

### Risk 2: Electrician Search Fails

**Likelihood:** Medium — Portland has electricians; finding one interested in a co-op is the challenge  
**Impact:** Co-op proceeds but with structural subcontractor dependency on highest-cost trade  
**Mitigation:**
- Three leads in parallel (Volt, Wren, Sparks) — increases probability of at least one conversion
- Backup A: expand search radius to Clackamas/Washington County
- Backup B: subcontract electrical Year 1, continue recruiting
- Backup C: if 5th slot cannot be an electrician, fill with highest-value alternative trade and keep electrician as a Year 1 addition target

### Risk 3: Capitalization Falls Short of $200K Target

**Likelihood:** High — moderate scenario projects $115K, not $200K  
**Impact:** May limit first acquisition to lower price point or require more aggressive leverage  
**Mitigation:**
- Confirm with Ledger that $115K is sufficient with 85% LTV hard money
- If not, consider: (a) increasing target buy-in for 1-2 members, (b) adding a 7th member, (c) adjusting first-project scope to match available capital
- Do not pressure candidates to buy in above their comfort level — that creates resentment and turnover risk

### Risk 4: EPA RRP Compliance Gap

**Likelihood:** Low-Medium — Birch's certification is lapsed; all others current or confirmed  
**Impact:** Birch cannot participate in project work until renewed  
**Mitigation:**
- Birch committed to 60-day renewal (by 2026-06-07)
- NW Environmental Training Center offers monthly refresher courses in Portland ($275)
- Track expiration dates for all members in a compliance calendar
- If electrician candidate lacks EPA RRP, same 60-day conditional applies

### Risk 5: Cultural Friction in Founding Cohort

**Likelihood:** Low — all three candidates show collaborative tendencies  
**Impact:** Could slow governance formation or create early conflicts  
**Mitigation:**
- Founders' meeting early (week 4) to test interpersonal chemistry
- Onboarding guide covers cooperative governance expectations
- Dispute resolution framework in operating agreement
- Birch and Copper have worked together before — existing relationship anchors the group
