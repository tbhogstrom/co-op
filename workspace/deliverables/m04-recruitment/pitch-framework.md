## Slide-by-Slide Framework

### Slide 1 — Hook / The Problem

**Title:** "You do the work. Someone else cashes the check."

**Content:**
- Portland real estate: median home price $520K+. Profit margins on a well-executed flip: $40,000–$120,000.
- Who captures that profit? Not the carpenter who reframed the walls. Not the roofer who made it watertight. Not the plumber who brought it to code.
- The person who holds the LLC — the developer, the investor, the GC with capital — captures it.
- Your labor creates that value. Your expertise makes the deal work. Your license is on the line.
- There's a different model.

**Why this works:** Opens with a frustration that every experienced tradesperson has felt. Makes them nod before we've said "co-op."

---

### Slide 2 — What Is a Housing Co-op?

**Title:** "A contractor co-op you actually own."

**Content:**
- The Portland Housing Co-op is a worker-owned entity that buys, rehabilitates, and sells residential properties in Portland.
- Members are not employees. Members are owner-operators.
- The co-op does what developers do — find the deal, finance it, plan the work, execute the rehab, sell — but the profit flows to the people who did the work, not to passive capital.
- Every member has a vote. Every member shares the upside.

**Analogy to use with union trades folks:** "It's like if the union hall bought the building instead of just representing the workers who built it."

## Delivery Notes

- **Audience**: Experienced tradespeople, not investors. Don't use financial jargon; use trade and labor language.
- **Format**: This should be delivered in person, ideally in small groups (5–8 people). One-on-ones for the founding members (Birch, Slate, Copper).
- **Tone**: Honest about risk, enthusiastic about potential, respectful of the audience's experience.
- **Length**: Keep the presentation under 20 minutes. Leave plenty of time for questions — the questions are where trust is built.
- **The closer**: Don't push for a commitment in the room. Ask them to "take a week, think about it, talk to me if you have more questions." The founders who come back with more questions are the ones who are serious.
