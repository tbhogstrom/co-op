Birch acknowledged the lapse without prompting and committed to renewal. Deadline: 60 days from today (2026-06-07). No project participation until confirmed.

In a co-op with democratic decisions, this could create friction if the group votes to reduce scope for budget reasons. Addressed in interview — Birch understands and accepts that the vote governs, even when he disagrees. Monitor during first project.

3. **Limited formal governance experience.** Union background gives him voting experience, but formal cooperative governance (board meetings, Robert's Rules-style process, fiduciary duties) will be new. Onboarding guide addresses this; plan to pair him with Maven for governance orientation.
