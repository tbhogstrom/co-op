## SECTION 5 — LABOR OBLIGATIONS AND COMPENSATION

*This Section applies to Working Members only. If the Member is admitted as an Investing Member, this Section does not apply except as to Section 5.8 (no Labor Share entitlement for Investing Members).*

**5.1 Participation Obligation.** The Member shall actively participate in the renovation and rehabilitation of Company Projects. The Member shall make good-faith efforts to contribute a minimum of ______ hours per month to Active Projects, subject to project scheduling and scope.

**5.2 Project Assignment.** The Member shall accept Project assignments as coordinated by the Board or Managing Member. The Member may decline a specific assignment for good cause (e.g., scheduling conflict with the Member's independent trade business, safety concern, work outside the Member's licensed trade), but a pattern of refusal without good cause may constitute grounds for expulsion under OA Section 5.9(b)(i).

**5.3 Time Tracking.** The Member shall record Labor Hours daily in the Company's time-tracking system. Entries must include: date, Project name, trade classification, hours worked (to the nearest quarter-hour), and brief description of work performed. (OA Section 8.3(a); Financial Exhibit B, Section B-3.)

**5.4 Weekly Verification.** Labor Hours shall be verified weekly by the Project Manager (or, during M1-M5, the Operational Director). Disputed hours shall be resolved by the Board. (OA Section 8.3(c); Financial Exhibit B, Section B-3(b).)

**5.5 Trade Classification and Rate.** The Member's primary trade classification and applicable Trade Rate multiplier are:

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Primary Trade Classification** | ________________________ |
| **Trade Rate Multiplier** | ______x |
| **Secondary Trade Classification(s)** (if any) | ________________________ |
| **Secondary Trade Rate(s)** | ______x |

Trade Rate multipliers per OA Exhibit B (as amended by Amendment #1) and Financial Exhibit B, Section B-2:

| Trade Classification | Trade Rate Multiplier |
|---------------------|-----------------------|
| Project Manager / General Contractor | 1.15x |
| Licensed Plumber | 1.3x |
| Licensed Electrician | 1.3x |
| Licensed HVAC Technician | 1.3x |
| Roofing | 1.2x |
| Carpenter / Framing | 1.2x |
| Painting / Finishing / Drywall | 1.0x |
| General Labor | 1.0x |
| Operations / Admin | 1.0x |

If the Member performs work in multiple trade categories on a single Project, hours shall be logged under the applicable trade for each task. The Trade Rate for each logged hour corresponds to the trade under which it is logged. (OA Section 8.3(b).)

**5.6 Weighted Labor Hours.** The Member's Weighted Labor Hours for each Project shall be calculated as:

> Weighted Labor Hours = Sum of (Hours in Trade Category x Trade Rate Multiplier)

(OA Section 1.29; Financial Exhibit B, Section B-3(c).)

**5.7 Labor Advances.** During the rehabilitation period of an Active Project, the Member may request a Labor Advance subject to the terms of OA Section 8.4 and Financial Exhibit C. Key terms:

(a) **Maximum advance:** Up to fifty percent (50%) of the Member's estimated Labor Share for the Project, calculated using the conservative gross profit scenario only. (OA Section 8.4(a); Financial Exhibit C, Section C-1.)

(b) **Completion gate:** No advance until the Project has reached at least thirty percent (30%) completion of the approved rehabilitation scope of work, as certified by the Project Manager or RMI. (OA Section 8.4(b-1); Financial Exhibit C, Section C-2.)

(c) **Approval authority:** Requests up to $3,000/month may be approved by the Treasurer; requests exceeding $3,000/month require Board approval. (OA Section 8.4(c); Financial Exhibit C, Section C-3.)

(d) **Minimum Reserve gate:** No advance if it would cause the Company's cash balance to fall below the Minimum Reserve. (OA Section 8.4(e); Financial Exhibit C, Section C-3.)

(e) **Nature of advances:** Labor Advances are advances against anticipated profit distributions. They are not wages, salary, draws, loans, or compensation for employment. (OA Section 8.4(f).)

(f) **Deduction:** Advances shall be deducted from the Member's final Labor Share distribution upon Project completion. (OA Section 8.4(d).)

**5.8 Clawback.** If the Member's final Labor Share is less than total Labor Advances received, or if the Project results in a loss:

(a) The excess advance amount shall first be deducted from the Member's Capital Account.

(b) If the Capital Account balance is insufficient, the Member shall repay the difference to the Company within **sixty (60) days**. (OA Section 8.4(d); Financial Exhibit C, Section C-4.)

(c) Any remaining unpaid balance after sixty (60) days becomes a receivable on the Company's books. The Member may not receive further Labor Advances or distributions until the balance is repaid in full. (Financial Exhibit C, Section C-4(c).)

**5.9 Investing Member — No Labor Share.** An Investing Member contributes no labor and receives no Labor Share. An Investing Member who contributes zero (0) Labor Hours to a Project shall receive only the Member's Capital Share. (OA Section 8.3(d).)

**5.10 Independent Contractor Status.** The Member is an independent business person and owner of the Company. The Member is not an employee. Nothing in this Agreement or the Operating Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship. The Member controls the manner and means of performing trade work, subject to Project-level coordination. (OA Article XV.)

## SECTION 6 — PROFIT DISTRIBUTION

**6.1 Profit Allocation Formula.** Gross Profit from each completed Project shall be allocated as follows (OA Section 8.2; Financial Exhibit B, Section B-1):

| Bucket | Percentage | Recipient | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Reserves** | 20% | Co-op reserve fund | Future acquisitions, contingencies, operating reserve |
| **Overhead Recovery** | 10% | Co-op operating account | Reimburses fixed overhead costs |
| **Capital Share** | 30% | Members, pro rata by Capital Account balance | Return on invested capital |
| **Labor Share** | 40% | Members, pro rata by Weighted Labor Hours | Return on sweat equity |
| **Total** | **100%** | | |

Amendment of the profit allocation formula requires Unanimous Vote. (OA Section 6.2(c).)

**6.2 Capital Share Calculation.** The Member's Capital Share for each Project shall be:

> Member's Capital Share = 30% x Gross Profit x (Member's Capital Account Balance / Total of All Members' Capital Account Balances)

Capital Account balances are measured as of the first day of the Project (date of property acquisition). (OA Section 8.2(c).)

**6.3 Labor Share Calculation.** The Member's Labor Share for each Project shall be:

> Member's Labor Share = 40% x Gross Profit x (Member's Weighted Labor Hours / Total of All Members' Weighted Labor Hours for the Project)

(OA Section 8.2(d); Financial Exhibit B, Section B-3(d).)

**6.4 Distribution Timing.** Capital Share and Labor Share distributions shall be made within thirty (30) days of the closing of the sale of a Project property and the Company's receipt of sale proceeds. (OA Section 8.5(a); Financial Exhibit B, Section B-4.)

**6.5 Distribution Statement.** The Treasurer shall prepare a distribution statement for each Project showing: Gross Profit, the four-way allocation, each Member's Capital Share, each Member's Labor Share, any Labor Advance deductions, and each Member's net distribution. (OA Section 8.5(b); Financial Exhibit B, Section B-4(b).)

**6.6 Loss Allocation.** If a Project results in a loss, the loss shall be allocated to the Member's Capital Account proportionally based on the Member's Capital Account balance relative to all Members' Capital Account balances. The Member shall not be required to make additional Capital Contributions on account of losses. No Member's Capital Account shall be reduced below zero. (OA Section 8.6.)

**6.7 Tax Treatment.** The Member acknowledges that:

(a) The Company is taxed as a partnership (Form 1065);

(b) The Member's distributive share of Company income will be reported on Schedule K-1, furnished within seventy-five (75) days after the close of each Fiscal Year (OA Section 8.7);

(c) The Member is solely responsible for all federal and state income taxes, self-employment taxes (IRC Section 1401 et seq.), and estimated tax payments arising from the Member's distributive share (OA Section 15.3);

(d) The Company shall not withhold income taxes, FICA, or any other employment taxes from distributions (OA Section 8.8).

## SECTION 7 — WITHDRAWAL AND TRANSFER

**7.1 Voluntary Withdrawal.** The Member may withdraw from the Company by providing **ninety (90) days'** prior written notice to the Board. (OA Section 5.8; Financial Exhibit D, Section D-1.)

**7.2 No Withdrawal During Active Projects.** The Member shall not withdraw during an Active Project in which the Member has contributed Labor Hours. If the Company has an Active Project on the date the withdrawal notice would otherwise become effective, the effective date of withdrawal shall be deferred to the earlier of: (i) thirty (30) days after the Project sale closes; or (ii) twelve (12) months from the date of the withdrawal notice. During the deferral period, the Member's Labor Hours continue to be tracked and the Member remains eligible for distributions from that Project. (OA Section 4.7(b); Financial Exhibit D, Section D-5.)

**7.3 Capital Return — Voluntary Withdrawal.** Upon effective withdrawal, the Member's Capital Account balance shall be paid within **sixty (60) days** of the effective date of withdrawal. If the Board determines that a lump-sum payment would create undue financial hardship for the Company, the Board may elect to pay in installments over twelve (12) months. (OA Section 4.7(d); Financial Exhibit D, Section D-1.)

**7.4 Minimum Reserve Gate.** The Board may deny or defer a withdrawal request if payment would cause the Company's cash balance to fall below the Minimum Reserve ($20,000). (OA Section 4.7(c); Financial Exhibit D, Section D-1.)

**7.5 Accrued Distributions.** Accrued but unpaid distributions for completed Projects in which the Member contributed Labor Hours shall be paid on the normal distribution schedule. (OA Section 5.8(b); Financial Exhibit D, Section D-1(a).)

**7.6 Involuntary Termination / Expulsion.** The Member may be expelled from the Company only for Cause and by Supermajority Vote, subject to the due process requirements of OA Section 5.9.

(a) **"Cause"** includes: (i) material breach of the OA or this Agreement uncured after thirty (30) days' written notice; (ii) loss, suspension, or revocation of CCB license; (iii) loss or lapse of required insurance uncured after fourteen (14) days' written notice; (iv) felony conviction or crime involving dishonesty; (v) willful misconduct or gross negligence on a Company Project; (vi) fraud, theft, or misappropriation of Company funds or property; (vii) violation of workplace safety standards creating substantial risk of injury. (OA Section 5.9(b).)

(b) **Due Process.** Before any expulsion vote, the Member shall receive: (i) written notice specifying grounds at least thirty (30) days before the scheduled vote; (ii) the opportunity to submit a written response; (iii) the opportunity to be heard at a meeting before the vote. (OA Section 5.9(c).)

**7.7 Capital Return — Expulsion.** If expelled, the Member's Capital Account balance shall be paid within **one hundred twenty (120) days** (lump sum) or in installments over **twelve (12) months**, at the Board's election. (OA Section 5.9(d); OA Section 11.3; Financial Exhibit D, Section D-2.)

**7.8 Forfeiture Upon Expulsion.** The expelled Member forfeits any Labor Share allocation for any Active Project that has not yet reached final sale closing. (OA Section 5.9(d); Financial Exhibit D, Section D-2.)

**7.9 Involuntary Transfer (Death, Disability, Bankruptcy).** In the event of the Member's death, disability, bankruptcy, or other involuntary transfer, the Company shall have the option to purchase the Member's Membership Interest at Capital Account balance as of the date of the triggering event. Payment shall be made within one hundred twenty (120) days or in installments over twelve (12) months, at the Board's election. (OA Section 11.3; Financial Exhibit D, Section D-3.)

**7.10 Deductions from Capital Return.** Before any capital return payment, the following amounts shall be deducted from the Member's Capital Account balance (Financial Exhibit D, Section D-4):

(a) Outstanding Labor Advances exceeding the Member's final Labor Share;

(b) Any amounts owed to the Company, including expense reimbursements, equipment, or tools;

(c) Allocated losses on the Member's Capital Account;

(d) Reasonable documented damages if the Member's departure causes demonstrable financial harm to an Active Project (subject to the Member's right to dispute).

**7.11 Restriction on Transfer.** No Member may sell, assign, transfer, pledge, or otherwise encumber any portion of the Member's Membership Interest without prior written consent of a Supermajority of the other Members. (OA Section 11.1.)

**7.12 Right of First Refusal.** Before any transfer to a third party, the Member shall first offer the interest to the Company (thirty (30) days to accept), then to existing Members pro rata (additional thirty (30) days). (OA Section 11.2.)

**7.13 No Transfer of Governance Rights.** An assignee of economic rights does not become a Member or acquire voting rights unless admitted pursuant to OA Section 5.3. (OA Section 11.4.)

## SECTION 8 — NON-COMPETITION

**8.1 Restricted Activity.** During the term of the Member's membership and for **twelve (12) months** following voluntary withdrawal or expulsion, the Member shall not, directly or through any entity, acquire, renovate, and sell ("flip") residential real property for the purpose of resale at a profit within the Portland metropolitan area (Multnomah, Clackamas, and Washington counties). (OA Section 15.6(a).)

**8.2 Permitted Activities.** This restriction does not apply to:

(a) The Member's personal residence;

(b) Performing trade work as an independent contractor for other persons or entities — Members are expressly permitted to maintain their own trade businesses and serve other clients;

(c) Passive investment in real estate (e.g., rental properties, REITs);

(d) Real property transactions outside the Portland metropolitan area; or

(e) Activity approved in writing by the Board. The Board shall not unreasonably withhold such approval.

(OA Section 15.6(b)-(c).)

**8.3 Narrow Tailoring.** This provision is narrowly tailored to protect the Company's legitimate business interests. It restricts only the specific activity of house flipping in a defined geography for a limited duration. It does not restrict the Member's ability to practice the Member's trade or earn a living.

**8.4 Enforceability.** This non-compete applies to the Member in the Member's capacity as an owner of the Company, not as an employee. ORS 653.295 (employee non-compete statute) does not apply. Enforceability is governed by general contract principles and the reasonableness standard applied by Oregon courts. (OA Section 15.6(d).)

**8.5 Member Acknowledgment.** The Member acknowledges that:

(a) The restriction is reasonable in scope, geography, and duration;

(b) The Member has received adequate consideration (membership in the Company, access to deal flow, profit-sharing rights) in exchange for this restriction; and

(c) A breach of this Section would cause irreparable harm to the Company, and the Company would be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

**11.6 Attorneys' Fees.** In any action, suit, arbitration, or proceeding to enforce or interpret this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the non-prevailing party. (OA Section 14.6.)
