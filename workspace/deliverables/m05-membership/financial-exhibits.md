## EXHIBIT A — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION SCHEDULE

### A-1. Buy-In Tiers

| Tier | Contribution Range | Description | Rights | Obligations |
|------|-------------------|-------------|--------|-------------|
| **Tier A — Full Share** | $25,000 - $50,000 | Full capital participation | Pro-rata Capital Share (30% bucket); one vote; eligible for all labor opportunities; eligible for Board service | Full buy-in due at signing (founding members); maintain active participation or notify Board of inactive status |
| **Tier B — Working Share** | $10,000 - $24,999 | Moderate capital participation | Pro-rata Capital Share (30% bucket); one vote; eligible for all labor opportunities; eligible for Board service | Full buy-in due at signing (founding members); maintain active participation or notify Board of inactive status |
| **Tier C — Sweat Equity Entry** | $5,000 | Minimum entry; primary wealth-building through labor | Pro-rata Capital Share (30% bucket); one vote; eligible for all labor opportunities; eligible for Board service | Full buy-in due at signing (founding members); maintain active participation or notify Board of inactive status |

**Minimum buy-in:** $5,000. **Maximum buy-in:** $50,000. These limits may be amended only by Supermajority Vote per OA Section 4.2.

### A-2. Payment Terms

(a) **Founding Members.** Capital Contributions are due in full at signing of the Member Agreement. No payment plans are available for founding members.

(b) **Future Members.** The Board may, by majority vote, offer payment plans to members admitted after founding. Terms of any such plan shall be documented in the member's individual Member Agreement.

(c) **Form of Payment.** Capital Contributions shall be made in cash (wire transfer, certified check, or ACH). Contributions in other property require Board approval and shall be valued at fair market value as determined by the Board (per OA Section 4.1).

### A-3. Capital Account Mechanics

Each member's Capital Account is maintained by the Treasurer and tracked as follows:

```
Capital Account Balance =
    Initial Capital Contribution
  + Additional Contributions (voluntary, Board-approved)
  + Allocated Profit (Capital Share + Labor Share from each completed Project)
  - Distributions Paid (cash paid to member)
  - Withdrawals (capital returned to member upon exit)
  = Current Balance
```

**Key rules:**

| Rule | Detail | OA Reference |
|------|--------|-------------|
| No interest on capital | Returns come from profit distributions, not interest | Section 4.4 |
| No additional contributions required | No member may be compelled to contribute beyond initial buy-in except by Unanimous Vote | Section 4.3 |
| Additional voluntary contributions | Accepted with Board approval; adjusts capital percentages for future Projects | Section 4.3 |
| Non-transferable | Capital accounts may not be transferred except to the Co-op | Section 11.1 |
| Monthly reporting | Treasurer provides Capital Account balance to each member monthly | Section 9.2 |

## EXHIBIT B — PROFIT DISTRIBUTION FORMULA

### B-1. The 20/10/30/40 Split

For each completed Project, Gross Profit is allocated as follows:

| Bucket | Percentage | Recipient | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Reserves** | 20% | Co-op reserve fund | Future down payments, contingencies, operating reserve |
| **Overhead Recovery** | 10% | Co-op operating account | Reimburses fixed overhead costs incurred during the Project cycle |
| **Capital Share** | 30% | Members, pro rata by Capital Account balance | Return on invested capital |
| **Labor Share** | 40% | Members, pro rata by Weighted Labor Hours | Return on sweat equity |
| **Total** | **100%** | | |

**Gross Profit** = Sale Price minus Total Project Cost, where Total Project Cost includes: purchase price, rehab costs (materials and subcontractors), acquisition costs (inspection, title, appraisal, origination), carrying costs (loan interest, taxes, insurance, utilities), selling costs (commissions, closing, staging, photography), and permits and miscellaneous.

### B-2. Trade Rate Multiplier Table

Per OA Exhibit B (as amended by Amendment #1):

| Trade Classification | Trade Rate Multiplier | Applicable To |
|---------------------|-----------------------|---------------|
| Project Manager / General Contractor | 1.15x | Members with GC license overseeing project operations |
| Licensed Plumber | 1.3x | Members with journeyman or master plumber license |
| Licensed Electrician | 1.3x | Members with journeyman or master electrician license |
| Licensed HVAC Technician | 1.3x | Members with HVAC license |
| Roofing | 1.2x | Members performing roofing work |
| Carpenter / Framing | 1.2x | Members performing structural carpentry |
| Painting / Finishing / Drywall | 1.0x | Members performing finish work |
| General Labor | 1.0x | Members performing non-specialized labor |
| Operations / Admin | 1.0x | Members performing administrative or operational tasks |

A member performing multiple trades on a single Project shall log hours under the applicable trade for each task. The Trade Rate for each logged hour corresponds to the trade under which it is logged.

### B-3. Labor Hour Tracking and Verification

(a) **Daily logging.** Each member logs hours daily, categorized by trade classification. Entries must include: date, Project name, trade classification, hours worked (to the nearest quarter-hour), and brief description of work performed.

(b) **Weekly verification.** The Project Manager (or, during M1-M5, the Operational Director) verifies all logged hours weekly. Disputed hours are resolved by the Board per OA Section 8.3(c).

(c) **Weighted hour calculation.** At Project completion, the Treasurer calculates each member's Weighted Labor Hours:

```
Weighted Labor Hours = Sum of (Hours in Trade Category x Trade Rate Multiplier)
                       for each trade category worked
```

(d) **Labor Share per member:**

```
Member's Labor Share % = Member's Weighted Hours / Total Weighted Hours (all members)
Member's Labor Share $  = Labor Share % x (40% x Gross Profit)
```

(e) **Final records.** The Treasurer maintains final records of all verified Labor Hours. These records are available for inspection by any member.

### B-4. Distribution Timing

(a) Capital Share and Labor Share distributions shall be made within **thirty (30) days** of the closing of the sale of a Project property and the Co-op's receipt of sale proceeds (per OA Section 8.5(a)).

(b) The Treasurer shall prepare a distribution statement for each Project showing: Gross Profit, the four-way allocation, each member's Capital Share, each member's Labor Share, any Labor Advance deductions, and each member's net distribution.

(c) Distributions shall be made by direct deposit or check to each member's designated account.

## EXHIBIT C — LABOR ADVANCE TERMS

### C-1. Maximum Advance

A member may draw up to **fifty percent (50%)** of the member's estimated Labor Share for an active Project as a Labor Advance (per OA Section 8.4(a)).

```
Maximum Advance = 50% x Estimated Labor Share

Where Estimated Labor Share =
    (Member's Estimated Weighted Hours / Total Estimated Weighted Hours)
    x (40% x Conservative Gross Profit Estimate)
```

The estimate must use the Treasurer's **conservative** gross profit projection. Moderate or aggressive scenarios shall not be used for advance calculations.

### C-2. Completion Gate

No Labor Advance shall be made until the Project has reached at least **thirty percent (30%)** completion of the approved rehabilitation scope of work, as certified by the Project Manager or Responsible Managing Individual (per OA Section 8.4(b-1), Amendment #2).

### C-3. Monthly Advance Limits and Approval

| Monthly Advance Request | Approval Authority | OA Reference |
|------------------------|--------------------|-------------|
| Up to $3,000 | Treasurer (sole approval) | Section 8.4(c) |
| Above $3,000 | Board vote required | Section 8.4(c) |

No Labor Advance shall be made if it would cause the Co-op's cash balance to fall below the Minimum Reserve (per OA Section 8.4(e)).

### C-4. Clawback Provision

If the Project results in a loss, or the member's final Labor Share is less than the total Labor Advances received:

(a) The excess advance amount shall first be deducted from the member's Capital Account.

(b) If the Capital Account balance is insufficient to cover the excess, the member shall repay the difference to the Co-op within **sixty (60) days** (per OA Section 8.4(d)).

(c) Any remaining unpaid balance after sixty (60) days becomes a receivable on the Co-op's books and accrues no interest, but the member may not receive further Labor Advances or distributions until the balance is repaid in full.

### C-5. Advance Tracking Process

(a) **Request.** Member submits a written advance request to the Treasurer specifying: amount requested, hours worked to date, trade classification, and estimated remaining hours.

(b) **Verification.** The Treasurer verifies that: (i) the 30% completion gate has been met; (ii) the requested amount does not exceed 50% of estimated Labor Share; (iii) the cumulative monthly amount is within the approval authority threshold; and (iv) the Co-op's cash balance will remain above the Minimum Reserve after payment.

(c) **Approval.** Treasurer approves (up to $3,000/month) or presents to the Board for vote (above $3,000/month).

(d) **Payment.** Approved advances are paid by direct deposit or check within five (5) business days of approval.

(e) **Logging.** Every advance is recorded in the advance tracker with: date, member name, amount, Project name, cumulative advance balance, and approval authority (Treasurer or Board).

(f) **Reconciliation.** At Project completion, total advances are deducted from the member's gross distribution. The Treasurer prepares a reconciliation statement showing: total advances drawn, final Labor Share, net amount due to (or from) the member.

## EXHIBIT D — WITHDRAWAL AND CAPITAL RETURN SCHEDULE

### D-1. Voluntary Withdrawal

| Term | Detail | OA Reference |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Notice period** | Ninety (90) days written notice to the Board | Section 5.8 |
| **Capital return timing** | Within sixty (60) days of the effective date of withdrawal | Section 5.8(d) |
| **Installment option** | If a lump-sum payment would create undue financial hardship for the Co-op, the Board may elect to pay in installments over twelve (12) months | Section 5.8(d) |
| **Amount returned** | Member's Capital Account balance as of the effective date of withdrawal | Section 5.8(d) |
| **Active Project restriction** | No withdrawal permitted during an Active Project in which the member has contributed Labor Hours, until the Project reaches final sale closing | Section 5.8 |
| **Minimum Reserve gate** | The Board may deny or defer a withdrawal request if payment would cause the Co-op's cash balance to fall below the Minimum Reserve ($20,000) | Section 5.8(c) |

(a) Accrued but unpaid distributions for completed Projects in which the member contributed Labor Hours shall be paid on the normal distribution schedule (per OA Section 5.8(b)).

(b) The withdrawing member shall have no further claim to Co-op profits or assets after the effective date of withdrawal (per OA Section 5.8(c)).

### D-2. Involuntary Withdrawal (Expulsion)

| Term | Detail | OA Reference |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Grounds** | For Cause only, by Supermajority Vote | Section 5.9(a) |
| **Due process** | 30 days written notice, opportunity to respond in writing, opportunity to be heard before vote | Section 5.9(c) |
| **Capital return timing** | Within one hundred twenty (120) days (lump sum) OR in installments over twelve (12) months, at the Board's election | Section 5.9(d), Section 11.3 |
| **Amount returned** | Member's Capital Account balance as of the effective date of expulsion | Section 5.9(d) |
| **Active Project forfeiture** | Expelled member forfeits any Labor Share allocation for any Active Project that has not yet reached final sale closing | Section 5.9(d) |

### D-3. Involuntary Transfer (Death, Disability, Bankruptcy)

| Term | Detail | OA Reference |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Co-op purchase option** | The Co-op has the option to purchase the member's Membership Interest at Capital Account balance as of the date of the triggering event | Section 11.3 |
| **Capital return timing** | Within one hundred twenty (120) days (lump sum) OR in installments over twelve (12) months, at the Board's election | Section 11.3 |

### D-4. Deductions from Capital Return

Before any capital return payment is made, the following amounts shall be deducted from the member's Capital Account balance:

| Deduction | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Outstanding Labor Advances** | Any Labor Advances received for Active Projects that exceed the member's final Labor Share (per OA Section 5.8(d)) |
| **Unpaid obligations** | Any amounts owed to the Co-op for any reason, including but not limited to expense reimbursements, equipment, or tools |
| **Allocated losses** | Any losses allocated to the member's Capital Account for Active or completed Projects |
| **Damages** | If the member's departure causes demonstrable financial harm to an Active Project (e.g., replacement labor costs, project delays), the Board may deduct reasonable documented damages from the capital return, subject to the member's right to dispute the amount |

### D-5. Restrictions on Withdrawal During Active Projects

(a) A member may not withdraw during an Active Project in which the member has contributed Labor Hours.

(b) If a member submits a 90-day withdrawal notice during an Active Project, the effective date of withdrawal is deferred until the earlier of: (i) thirty (30) days after the Project sale closes; or (ii) twelve (12) months from the date of the withdrawal notice.

(c) During the deferral period, the member's Labor Hours on the Active Project continue to be tracked and the member remains eligible for Labor Share and Capital Share distributions from that Project.

## EXHIBIT E — FIRST-YEAR FINANCIAL GUARDRAILS

Per the Minimum Viable Capitalization Analysis (M3 Deliverable), the following guardrails apply to the Co-op's first acquisition and first year of operations. These guardrails are binding constraints on Board action and may be amended only by Supermajority Vote.

### E-1. First Acquisition Constraints

| Constraint | Limit | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **Maximum purchase price** | $200,000 | Ensures adequate equity cushion at projected capitalization levels; targets Lents/Parkrose price range |
| **Maximum rehab budget** | $55,000 | Limits exposure to cosmetic-plus scope (kitchen, bath, paint, flooring, landscaping); avoids structural or major-system risk on first flip |

### E-2. Reserve Requirements Before First Acquisition

Before closing on the first property, the Treasurer shall certify that the following reserves are funded and segregated in the Reserve Savings account:

| Reserve Component | Minimum Amount | Basis |
|-------------------|---------------|-------|
| Operating reserve (3 months overhead) | $14,600 | Annual overhead ~$58,400 / 12 x 3 |
| Project contingency reserve (15% of rehab budget) | $9,000 | 15% x $60,000 estimated rehab budget |
| **Total locked reserves** | **$23,600** | |

These reserves are locked. No draw from these funds for project costs, operating expenses, or distributions. Any draw from reserves exceeding $5,000 requires majority Board vote, and if reserves are drawn below $23,600, the replenishment rate increases from 20% to 25% of the next Project's Gross Profit until restored.

### E-3. Parallel Project Restriction

The Co-op shall not commence a second Project (defined as entering into a purchase agreement) until the Reserve Fund balance reaches a minimum of **$40,000**. This ensures the Co-op can support two projects' combined contingency and operating costs without distress.

### E-4. Additional First-Year Safeguards

| Safeguard | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Inspection contingency** | Budget $2,000 for comprehensive inspection (general, sewer scope, structural) on first acquisition. Walk away from any property with discoveries that would push rehab budget above $55,000. |
| **Conservative ARV pricing** | Price the first flip to sell. Do not stretch ARV assumptions. Use comparable sales within 0.5 miles and 6 months. |
| **Continued recruitment** | Continue recruiting 1-2 additional members during the first flip cycle. Additional buy-ins flow to the balance sheet and expand capacity for the second acquisition. |
| **Scope control** | No change orders exceeding $2,500 without Board approval. Total change orders on first Project shall not exceed 15% of approved rehab budget ($8,250 on a $55,000 budget) without Supermajority Vote. |
