5. **May 20 - June 10 (Signing Window -- Tranche 3):** Execute Member Agreement for Volt (if vetting completes and Supermajority Vote is obtained).

### 4.2 Notary Requirements

The Member Agreement v2 template includes a Notary Acknowledgment page (page 487-500 of template). Oregon law does not require notarization of LLC operating agreements or member agreements. However, notarization is recommended for the following reasons:

- **Evidentiary value:** A notarized signature is prima facie evidence of authenticity under ORS 194.505.
- **Recording potential:** If the Member Agreement or a memorandum thereof is ever recorded with the county, notarization is required.
- **Dispute prevention:** Notarization eliminates future disputes about whether a signature is genuine.

**Recommendation:** Arrange for a mobile notary to be available at each signing session. Oregon mobile notary fees are typically $10-$25 per notarization. Budget for 5 member signings plus Company signatory = approximately $60-$150 total.

**Company Signatory:** The "By" line on the signature page designates "Managing Member / Board Chair." As of the signing date, Maven will likely sign on behalf of the Company. Confirm that the OA authorizes the Managing Member or Board Chair to execute Member Agreements on behalf of the Company.
