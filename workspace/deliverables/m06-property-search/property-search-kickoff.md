## 2. Deal Sourcing Strategy

We are sourcing from five channels, ranked by expected deal quality for our profile:

### Channel 1: MLS / Agent Network (Primary)

**What:** Active MLS listings in target neighborhoods matching our filters.
**How:** Weekly automated search alerts filtered to $130K-$210K list price, SFR, 2-3 BR, Lents/Cully/Parkrose zip codes (97266, 97218, 97220, 97230). Agent network contacts for pocket listings and pre-market deals.
**Expected yield:** 3-5 viable candidates per month.
**Lead time:** Properties can move to offer within 1-2 weeks of identification.

### Channel 2: Estate Sales (High Priority)

**What:** Properties being sold by heirs, often through probate or informal family sales.
**How:** Monitor probate filings in Multnomah County Circuit Court. Track obituary-to-property-record crossmatch for long-term homeowners in target neighborhoods. Network with estate attorneys and estate sale companies.
**Expected yield:** 1-2 viable candidates per month.
**Lead time:** 2-4 weeks (heirs often slow to decide, but motivated once they do).
**Why prioritized:** Clean title, no occupant complications, motivated sellers willing to accept below-market offers. Our best deals will likely come from this channel.

### Channel 3: Pre-Foreclosure / REO (Active)

**What:** Properties with Notice of Default filed or already bank-owned.
**How:** Monitor Multnomah County Recorder for NOD filings weekly. Track REO listings from major servicers (Wells Fargo, Bank of America, Nationstar). Contact pre-foreclosure homeowners via door-knock or letter campaign (after title research by Statton).
**Expected yield:** 1-3 viable candidates per month.
**Lead time:** Variable. Pre-foreclosure can be 30-90 days from contact to close. REO averages 45-60 days.

### Channel 4: Wholesalers (Opportunistic)

**What:** Off-market deals sourced by local wholesalers who assign contracts to buyers.
**How:** Build relationships with 3-5 Portland-area wholesalers active in outer East Portland. Review deal packages as they come in. Apply same screening criteria — most wholesale deals will not meet our standards.
**Expected yield:** 1 viable candidate per month (high volume, low hit rate).
**Lead time:** Fast — wholesalers need to close in 7-14 days. Good for us if we have capital ready.
**Caution:** Wholesale fees ($5K-$15K) compress margins. Only pursue if the deal still clears 65% rule after the assignment fee.

### Channel 5: Driving for Dollars / Direct Outreach (Supplemental)

**What:** Physically identifying distressed properties in target neighborhoods and contacting owners directly.
**How:** Systematic block-by-block survey of high-score zones in Lents, Cully, Parkrose. Identify properties showing distress signals: overgrown yards, boarded windows, deferred maintenance, code violation notices. Cross-reference owner information via Multnomah County Assessor and PortlandMaps. Send direct mail or door-knock.
**Expected yield:** 0-1 viable candidate per month.
**Lead time:** Long — 4-8 weeks from identification to potential deal. This is a slow-burn pipeline.
**Who:** Reeves scouts; members can contribute observations from their daily routes.

## 3. Evaluation Pipeline

Every property moves through a four-stage funnel. Properties are eliminated at the earliest possible stage to avoid wasting time and money on bad deals.

### Stage 1: Desktop Screen (Reeves — 30 minutes per property)

**Purpose:** Determine if the property is worth a physical visit.

**Actions:**
- Pull property record from Multnomah County Assessor (sqft, lot, year built, zoning, tax status)
- Check listing photos, disclosures, and agent remarks
- Run preliminary ARV estimate using comp-analyzer tool (3 comps minimum)
- Run preliminary deal score using deal-scorer tool
- Calculate 65% rule max purchase price
- Estimate rehab scope from photos and listing notes (cosmetic-plus check)
- Check for red flags: flood zone, environmental contamination, title liens, code violations

**Pass criteria:**
- Estimated purchase price (after negotiation) is at or below 65% rule max
- Rehab appears to be cosmetic-plus (no visible structural, roof, or major systems issues)
- Estimated rehab ≤ $55K based on photo assessment
- Estimated ARV supports ≥ 15% ROI
- No environmental or title red flags visible at desktop level
- Neighborhood score ≥ 65

**Output:** Desktop screen summary (1 page). GO / NO-GO / CONDITIONAL recommendation.

**Expected volume:** Screen 8-12 properties per week. Advance 2-3 to Stage 2.

### Stage 2: Drive-By Assessment (Reeves — 1-2 hours per property)

**Purpose:** Verify desktop assumptions with physical observation. Catch issues not visible in listing photos.

**Actions:**
- Drive by the property. Photograph exterior from all angles.
- Assess: roof condition (from ground), foundation visible condition, siding/paint condition, yard/landscaping state, window condition, garage/outbuilding condition
- Assess the block: neighboring properties condition, street quality, noise level, proximity to amenities/transit/nuisances
- Knock on neighbor doors if appropriate — ask about property history, owner situation, neighborhood dynamics
- Update ARV estimate based on physical neighborhood observation
- Update rehab scope estimate based on exterior condition

**Pass criteria:**
- Exterior condition is consistent with or better than listing description
- No visible structural red flags (foundation cracks, sagging roofline, significant water damage)
- Block quality supports ARV estimate
- Rehab scope still appears ≤ $55K cosmetic-plus

**Output:** Drive-by assessment memo with photos. Updated deal score. GO / NO-GO recommendation.

**Expected volume:** 2-3 drive-bys per week. Advance 1-2 to Stage 3.

### Stage 3: Walkthrough + Inspection (Reeves + Harlan — half day per property)

**Purpose:** Confirm rehab scope and cost. Identify any deal-killing issues before making an offer.

**Actions:**
- Schedule walkthrough with listing agent or seller
- Reeves and Harlan walk the property together
- Harlan assesses: all structural systems (foundation, framing, roof from attic if accessible), electrical (panel type, wiring type, capacity), plumbing (pipe material, visible leaks, water pressure), HVAC, kitchen/bath scope, flooring, walls
- Harlan produces preliminary scope of work and rehab estimate
- If rehab estimate > $45K, order professional inspection ($400-$600) to verify scope
- If property is pre-1978, assess lead paint and asbestos risk (visual + disclosure review)
- If any system looks questionable, budget $2,000 for comprehensive inspection (general + sewer scope + structural) per Ledger's guardrail

**Pass criteria:**
- Harlan's rehab estimate ≤ $55,000
- Scope is cosmetic-plus: kitchen, bath, paint, flooring, landscaping, minor repairs. No structural work, no full roof replacement, no full rewire, no full repipe
- No environmental hazards without clear, budgetable remediation
- No foundation issues
- No title or zoning complications (Statton confirms)
- Updated deal score ≥ 65

**Output:** Full deal analysis using deal-analysis-template.md. Harlan's scope of work and estimate. GO / NO-GO / CONDITIONAL recommendation with offer strategy.

**Expected volume:** 1-2 walkthroughs per week. Advance 0-1 to Stage 4.

### Stage 4: Offer (Maven + Reeves + Statton + Ledger)

**Purpose:** Make an offer and negotiate to contract.

**Actions:**
- Reeves presents deal analysis to Maven and board
- Board vote to proceed (2/3 supermajority required per governance rules)
- Statton prepares or reviews purchase agreement
- Ledger confirms capital availability and cash flow projection
- Reeves and Maven determine offer price, walk-away price, and negotiation strategy
- Submit offer through agent or directly to seller

**Offer structure:**
- Cash or hard-money offer with 14-21 day close (our competitive advantage)
- Inspection contingency (10-14 days)
- Financing contingency (if hard money, 10 days)
- Earnest money: $3,000-$5,000 (held by title company)

**Negotiation parameters:**
- Opening offer: 10-15% below target purchase price (if list price is above our max)
- Walk-away price: 65% rule max, firm
- Leverage: fast close, cash-like terms, no appraisal contingency (hard money doesn't require one)
- Timeline: expect 1-3 rounds of counteroffers over 5-10 days

**Output:** Executed purchase agreement or documented walk-away.
