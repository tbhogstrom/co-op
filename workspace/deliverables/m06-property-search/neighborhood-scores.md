## Methodology

Neighborhoods are scored on a 0-100 scale using nine weighted criteria aligned with the co-op's fix-and-flip strategy. Higher scores indicate better fit for our investment thesis: affordable entry, strong appreciation, available distressed inventory, and reliable exit demand.

### Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What We're Measuring |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| Entry Price vs Metro | 15% | Lower median price = easier entry = more deals in budget |
| 3-Year Appreciation | 20% | Stronger appreciation = higher ARV confidence |
| Days on Market | 10% | Faster absorption = lower holding risk |
| Distressed Inventory | 15% | More distressed = more deal flow |
| Crime Trend | 10% | Improving crime = appreciation catalyst |
| Transit Access | 10% | Better transit = broader buyer pool |
| School Rating | 5% | Higher rating = family buyer demand |
| Development Pipeline | 10% | Active investment = neighborhood momentum |
| Walkability/Amenities | 5% | Better walkability = higher desirability |

**Deal Threshold:** Combined score >= 65 to pursue
**Watch List:** Score 55-64
**Pass:** Score < 55
