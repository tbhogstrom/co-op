### ARTICLE IV — MANAGEMENT STRUCTURE

**Management Type:** Member-Managed

> The Oregon SOS online form asks whether the LLC is member-managed or manager-managed.
> Select **"Member-Managed"** per ORS 63.130. This is consistent with the Operating
> Agreement and the cooperative's democratic governance structure.

The Company shall be managed by its Members in accordance with the Operating Agreement. Each Member shall have one (1) vote on all matters submitted to a vote of the Members, regardless of the Member's capital account balance or percentage interest in the Company.

---

### ARTICLE V — PURPOSE

The Company is organized for the following purposes:

(a) To acquire, renovate, improve, and sell residential real property in the Portland, Oregon metropolitan area and the State of Oregon;

(b) To operate as a worker-owned cooperative enterprise in which Members contribute both capital and labor to the Company's business operations;

(c) To hold, manage, and dispose of real and personal property necessary or incidental to the Company's business;

(d) To obtain and maintain all licenses, permits, and registrations necessary to conduct construction and real estate activities in the State of Oregon, including without limitation licensure by the Oregon Construction Contractors Board pursuant to ORS 701.021;

(e) To engage in any and all lawful business activities for which a limited liability company may be organized under ORS Chapter 63.

The Company shall operate in accordance with cooperative principles, including democratic governance, equitable distribution of surplus based on patronage, and open membership, as further described in the Operating Agreement.

> **[FILING NOTE]** The Oregon SOS online form has a "purpose" field. Some filers enter
> only "any lawful purpose." For the Co-op, we recommend entering the full cooperative
> purpose statement above (or at minimum subsections (a), (b), and (e)) to establish the
> cooperative character of the entity on the public record. If the form has character
> limits, use this abbreviated version:
>
> *"To operate as a worker-owned cooperative enterprise that acquires, renovates, and
> sells residential real property in Oregon, and to engage in any lawful business."*

### ARTICLE VIII — ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

**8.1 Operating Agreement.** The rights, duties, and obligations of the Members and the governance of the Company shall be governed by a written Operating Agreement, as amended from time to time. In the event of a conflict between these Articles of Organization and the Operating Agreement, these Articles shall control as to third parties and the Operating Agreement shall control as between the Members, except as otherwise required by ORS Chapter 63.

**8.2 Liability Limitation.** No Member, manager, officer, or agent of the Company shall be personally liable for any debt, obligation, or liability of the Company solely by reason of being or acting as a Member, manager, officer, or agent of the Company, in accordance with ORS 63.165.

**8.3 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless each Member, manager, officer, and agent of the Company to the fullest extent permitted by ORS 63.160, as further set forth in the Operating Agreement.

> **[FILING NOTE]** The online form may have an "additional provisions" or "optional
> provisions" text field. Enter Sections 8.1 through 8.3 above. If the form lacks such
> a field, these provisions are operative through the Operating Agreement and the
> Articles as a whole. They do not need to appear in the online form to be effective,
> but filing them on the public record strengthens their enforceability against third
> parties.
