## Purpose

This checklist provides a step-by-step guide for filing the Co-op's entity-level CCB license application and clarifies individual member licensing obligations. All steps should be completed before the Co-op begins any renovation work.

---

## Part 1 — Entity CCB License Application

### 1.1 Pre-Filing Requirements

- [ ] **Form the LLC.** Articles of Organization must be filed and accepted by the Oregon Secretary of State (ORS 63.044) before applying for a CCB license. Obtain the entity's registry number.
- [ ] **Obtain an EIN.** File IRS Form SS-4 (or apply online at irs.gov) to obtain a federal Employer Identification Number for Portland Housing Cooperative, LLC.
- [ ] **Designate the Responsible Managing Individual (RMI).** The Board shall designate one Member to serve as RMI per Section 7.8(d) of the Operating Agreement. The RMI must:
  - Hold an active individual Oregon CCB license (or qualify by exam)
  - Pass the CCB pre-license education requirements and exam (if not already licensed)
  - Have authority over the construction activities of the entity
  - **Recommendation:** Maven (Founder/Operations) should serve as initial RMI, subject to Board confirmation
- [ ] **RMI completes pre-license training.** If the designated RMI does not already hold a CCB license, the RMI must complete CCB-approved pre-license training (16 hours for general contractors). Training providers are listed on the CCB website.
- [ ] **RMI passes CCB exam.** If required, the RMI must pass the Oregon CCB licensing exam.

### 1.2 Insurance — Obtain Before Filing

- [ ] **General Liability Insurance.** Obtain a commercial general liability (CGL) policy for the entity:
  - Minimum: $500,000 per occurrence / $1,000,000 aggregate (OAR 812-003-0175)
  - **Recommended for co-op:** $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate (per OA Section 10.2(a))
  - Insurer must be authorized to do business in Oregon
  - Obtain ACORD certificate of insurance naming the CCB as certificate holder
- [ ] **Workers Compensation Insurance.** Determine obligation:
  - If all members are bona fide co-op members (not employees): may be exempt, but coverage is strongly recommended (per OA Section 10.2(b))
  - If the co-op hires any employees or day laborers: mandatory (ORS Chapter 656)
  - Obtain policy or exemption documentation
- [ ] **Surety Bond.** Obtain a $20,000 surety bond for the entity (residential general contractor):
  - Bond must be from a surety company authorized in Oregon
  - Bond form must comply with CCB requirements
  - Bond must name the CCB as obligee

### 1.3 Filing the Application

- [ ] **Complete CCB Application Form.** Download from https://www.oregon.gov/ccb or request by mail. Select:
  - License type: **General Contractor**
  - License category: **Residential**
  - Entity type: **LLC**
- [ ] **Provide entity information:**
  - Legal name: Portland Housing Cooperative, LLC
  - Oregon Secretary of State registry number
  - EIN
  - Principal office address (Portland, OR)
  - Mailing address
  - Phone and email
- [ ] **Provide RMI information:**
  - Full legal name
  - Individual CCB license number (if applicable)
  - Date of birth
  - Social Security Number (required by CCB)
  - Proof of pre-license education and exam passage
- [ ] **Attach required documents:**
  - Proof of general liability insurance (ACORD certificate)
  - Proof of surety bond
  - Proof of workers compensation insurance (or exemption affidavit)
  - Copy of Articles of Organization (filed with Secretary of State)
- [ ] **Pay application fee.** Current fee schedule (verify at time of filing):
  - Initial license fee: approximately $325 (residential general contractor)
  - Fees are subject to change; check current CCB fee schedule
- [ ] **Submit application.** File with the Oregon Construction Contractors Board:
  - Online: https://www.oregon.gov/ccb
  - Mail: CCB, 201 High St SE, Suite 600, Salem, OR 97301
  - Phone: (503) 378-4621

### 1.4 Post-Filing

- [ ] **Track application status.** The CCB typically processes applications within 2-4 weeks.
- [ ] **Receive CCB license number.** Upon approval, record the entity's CCB license number and expiration date.
- [ ] **Display license.** Post the CCB license number in all advertising, contracts, and at job sites (ORS 701.305).
- [ ] **Set renewal reminder.** CCB licenses must be renewed every two years. Set a calendar reminder 60 days before expiration.

## Part 2 — EPA RRP Firm Certification

Portland's housing stock is predominantly pre-1978. The entity must be certified as an EPA renovation firm before performing work on pre-1978 structures.

- [ ] **Apply for EPA RRP Firm Certification.** File with EPA Region 10 (or through Oregon DEQ if delegated):
  - Application fee: approximately $300 (verify at time of filing)
  - Certification is valid for 5 years
- [ ] **Designate at least one EPA-certified renovator.** At least one member on each job site must hold an individual EPA RRP Renovator certification (8-hour initial training course).
- [ ] **Establish lead-safe work practices.** Document procedures for containment, cleaning, and verification per 40 CFR 745, Subpart E.

## Part 3 — Individual Member Licensing

### 3.1 Who Needs an Individual CCB License?

Under the Co-op's structure (members as independent contractor-members performing work on properties the Co-op intends to sell), **every Working Member performing construction, renovation, or trade work must hold their own individual CCB license** (ORS 701.021). The owner-builder exemption (ORS 701.026(1)(a)) does not apply because the properties are acquired with the intent to sell.

| Member Type | Individual CCB License Required? |
|-------------|----------------------------------|
| Working Member — general construction | Yes (General Contractor, Residential) |
| Working Member — specialty trade (plumbing, electrical, HVAC) | Yes (CCB license + applicable state trade license) |
| Investing Member (no construction labor) | No |
| Operations / Admin only (no on-site construction) | No, unless performing any construction-related activity |

### 3.2 Individual Member Checklist

Each Working Member should complete the following before beginning work on any Co-op Project:

- [ ] Hold active Oregon CCB license (general or specialty) — verified via CCB online lookup
- [ ] Maintain general liability insurance ($500,000/$1,000,000 minimum)
- [ ] Maintain surety bond per CCB requirements ($20,000 residential)
- [ ] Obtain EPA RRP Renovator certification (if performing work on pre-1978 structures)
- [ ] Obtain applicable state trade licenses (plumbing, electrical, HVAC — these are separate from CCB)
- [ ] Provide copies of all licenses, insurance certificates, and bond documentation to the Company Secretary
- [ ] Complete and sign Member Agreement Exhibit B (CCB License and Insurance Information)

### 3.3 Members Working Under the Entity License

A member does **not** need a separate CCB license if:
- The member is classified as an **employee** of the co-op (working under the entity's license)

However, under the Co-op's chosen structure (independent contractor-members), this exception generally does not apply. All Working Members should hold individual licenses. If the Co-op later restructures the employment relationship, this analysis should be revisited.
