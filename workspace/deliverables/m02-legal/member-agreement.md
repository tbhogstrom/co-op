## RECITALS

A. The Company is a worker-owned cooperative limited liability company organized under ORS Chapter 63 for the purpose of acquiring, renovating, and selling distressed residential real property in the Portland, Oregon metropolitan area.

B. The Company has adopted an Operating Agreement dated ____________, 2026 (the "Operating Agreement"), which governs the rights, duties, and obligations of the Company's Members.

C. The Member desires to be admitted as a [Working / Investing] Member of the Company and to participate in the Company's operations in accordance with the Operating Agreement and this Member Agreement.

D. This Member Agreement is the individual agreement referenced in Section 5.6 of the Operating Agreement and sets forth the specific terms of the Member's engagement with the Company.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Member Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Operating Agreement.

## SECTION 1 — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION

**1.1 Commitment.** The Member shall make an initial Capital Contribution to the Company in the amount of **$____________** (the "Contribution Amount"), which falls within the range established in Section 4.2 of the Operating Agreement (minimum $5,000; maximum $50,000).

**1.2 Payment.** The Contribution Amount shall be paid in full, in cash or immediately available funds, on or before ____________, 2026 (the "Contribution Deadline"). No payment plan is available for founding Members. The Member's admission to the Company is contingent upon receipt of the full Contribution Amount by the Contribution Deadline.

**1.3 Capital Account.** The Company shall establish and maintain a Capital Account for the Member in accordance with Article IV of the Operating Agreement. The Member's Capital Account shall be credited, debited, and maintained as set forth therein and in compliance with Treasury Regulation Section 1.704-1(b)(2)(iv).

**1.4 No Interest; No Guaranteed Return.** The Member shall not receive interest on the Contribution Amount or Capital Account balance. Returns, if any, come solely from profit distributions under Article VIII of the Operating Agreement.

**1.5 Capital Contribution Schedule.** The specific amount and payment date are set forth in **Exhibit A** attached hereto.

## SECTION 2 — LABOR COMMITMENT

**2.1 Participation Obligation.** If admitted as a Working Member, the Member shall actively participate in the renovation and rehabilitation of Company Projects. The Member shall make good-faith efforts to contribute a minimum of ______ hours per month to Active Projects, subject to project scheduling and scope.

**2.2 Project Assignment.** The Member shall accept Project assignments as coordinated by the Board or Managing Member. The Member may decline a specific assignment for good cause (e.g., scheduling conflict with the Member's independent trade business, safety concern, work outside the Member's licensed trade), but a pattern of refusal without good cause may constitute grounds for expulsion under Section 5.9(b)(i) of the Operating Agreement.

**2.3 Time Tracking.** The Member shall record Labor Hours daily in the Company's time-tracking system, categorized by trade classification, in accordance with Section 8.3 of the Operating Agreement.

**2.4 Trade Classification.** The Member's primary trade classification is: **________________________**. The applicable Trade Rate multiplier is: **______x** (per the Trade Rate Schedule in Exhibit B of the Operating Agreement). If the Member performs work in multiple trade categories on a single Project, hours shall be logged under the applicable trade for each task.

## SECTION 3 — CCB LICENSING OBLIGATION

**3.1 License Requirement.** As a condition of membership, the Member shall obtain and maintain, at the Member's sole expense, a valid Oregon CCB license appropriate to the Member's trade, in good standing with no unresolved disciplinary actions, pursuant to ORS 701.021.

**3.2 Insurance and Bonding.** The Member shall maintain, at the Member's sole expense:

(a) General liability insurance with minimum limits of $500,000 per occurrence and $1,000,000 aggregate (per OAR 812-003-0175);

(b) A surety bond as required by the CCB for the Member's license type ($20,000 minimum for residential contractors); and

(c) Workers compensation coverage if the Member employs any persons.

**3.3 EPA RRP Certification.** If the Member will perform renovation work on structures built before 1978, the Member shall obtain and maintain current EPA Renovation, Repair, and Painting Rule certification (40 CFR 745, Subpart E).

**3.4 Annual Verification.** The Member shall provide current certificates of insurance, proof of bond, and CCB license documentation to the Company Secretary annually and upon each renewal. The Member's CCB license number and insurance information are set forth in **Exhibit B** attached hereto.

**3.5 Lapse Consequences.** Loss, suspension, revocation, or lapse of the Member's CCB license or required insurance constitutes grounds for expulsion under Section 5.9(b)(ii)-(iii) of the Operating Agreement.

## SECTION 4 — PROFIT AND LOSS ALLOCATION

**4.1 Profit Allocation.** The Member acknowledges and agrees that Gross Profit from each Project shall be allocated in accordance with Article VIII of the Operating Agreement:

| Bucket | Percentage |
|--------|-----------|
| Reserve Contribution | 20% |
| Overhead Recovery | 10% |
| Capital Share | 30% (pro rata by Capital Account balance) |
| Labor Share | 40% (pro rata by Weighted Labor Hours) |

**4.2 Loss Allocation.** If a Project results in a loss, the loss shall be allocated to the Member's Capital Account proportionally based on the Member's Capital Account balance relative to all Members' Capital Account balances, pursuant to Section 8.6 of the Operating Agreement. The Member shall not be required to make additional Capital Contributions on account of losses.

**4.3 Tax Treatment.** The Member acknowledges that the Company is taxed as a partnership (Form 1065) and that the Member's distributive share of Company income will be reported on Schedule K-1. The Member is solely responsible for all federal and state income taxes, self-employment taxes (IRC Section 1401 et seq.), and estimated tax payments arising from the Member's distributive share.

---

## SECTION 5 — LABOR ADVANCE ELIGIBILITY

**5.1 Eligibility.** During the rehabilitation period of an Active Project, the Member may request a Labor Advance of up to fifty percent (50%) of the Member's estimated Labor Share for that Project, subject to the conditions set forth in Section 8.4 of the Operating Agreement and this Section 5.

**5.2 Conditions.** Labor Advances are available only when all of the following conditions are met:

(a) The Project has reached at least thirty percent (30%) completion of the approved rehabilitation scope of work, as determined by the Project manager or RMI;

(b) The Member's estimated Labor Share is calculated using the conservative gross profit scenario only (not the moderate or aggressive scenario);

(c) The Board (or Treasurer, for advances not exceeding $3,000/month) has approved the advance request;

(d) The Company's cash balance, after giving effect to the advance, will not fall below the Minimum Reserve; and

(e) The Member's verified Labor Hours to date support the estimated Labor Share calculation.

**5.3 Nature of Advances.** Labor Advances are advances against anticipated profit distributions. They are not wages, salary, draws, loans, or compensation for employment. They shall be deducted from the Member's final Labor Share distribution upon Project completion.

**5.4 Clawback.** If the Member's final Labor Share is less than total Labor Advances received, or if the Project results in a loss, the Member shall repay the excess to the Company within sixty (60) days. Any unpaid balance shall be deducted from the Member's Capital Account. If the Capital Account is insufficient, the remaining balance becomes a receivable payable by the Member within ninety (90) days.

## SECTION 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Confidential Information.** The Member shall not disclose to any third party, during or after membership, any Confidential Information of the Company. "Confidential Information" includes: (a) financial records, projections, and profit margins; (b) property acquisition targets, bid amounts, and deal terms; (c) member capital account balances and distribution amounts; (d) proprietary renovation processes, cost estimates, and supplier pricing; and (e) any other information designated as confidential by the Board.

**6.2 Exceptions.** This obligation does not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Member; (b) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order; or (c) is disclosed to the Member's own legal, tax, or financial advisors under a duty of confidentiality.

**6.3 Duration.** The confidentiality obligation survives termination of membership for a period of three (3) years.

---

## SECTION 7 — NON-COMPETE

**7.1 Restricted Activity.** During the term of the Member's membership and for twelve (12) months following voluntary withdrawal or expulsion, the Member shall not, directly or through any entity, acquire, renovate, and sell ("flip") residential real property for the purpose of resale at a profit within the Target Neighborhoods identified by the Company's acquisition strategy (as maintained by the Board and communicated to Members from time to time) or within a one-mile radius thereof.

**7.2 Permitted Activities.** This restriction does not apply to:

(a) The Member's personal residence;

(b) Performing trade work as an independent contractor for other persons or entities (Members may maintain their own trade businesses and serve other clients);

(c) Passive investment in real estate (e.g., rental properties, REITs);

(d) Real property transactions outside the Target Neighborhoods and their one-mile radius; or

(e) Activity approved in writing by the Board.

**7.3 Narrow Tailoring.** This provision is narrowly tailored to protect the Company's legitimate business interests in its specific target markets. It restricts only the specific activity of house flipping in specific neighborhoods for a limited duration. It does not restrict the Member's ability to practice the Member's trade or earn a living. The Member acknowledges that this restriction is reasonable in scope, geography, and duration.

**7.4 Enforceability Note.** This non-compete applies to the Member in the Member's capacity as an owner of the Company, not as an employee. ORS 653.295 (employee non-compete statute) does not apply. Enforceability is governed by general contract principles and the reasonableness standard applied by Oregon courts.

## SECTION 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**8.1 Informal Resolution.** The Member and the Company shall first attempt to resolve any dispute arising under this Member Agreement or the Operating Agreement through informal discussion with the Board.

**8.2 Mediation.** If informal resolution is unsuccessful within fifteen (15) days, the parties shall submit the dispute to mediation administered by a mutually agreed mediator in Portland, Oregon. The Company shall bear the cost of mediation.

**8.3 Binding Arbitration.** If mediation is unsuccessful within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Portland, Oregon, administered by the Arbitration Service of Portland (or a comparable local arbitration service), in accordance with the Oregon Uniform Arbitration Act (ORS Chapter 36). The arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

**8.4 Venue.** Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon.

**8.5 Attorneys' Fees.** The prevailing party in any dispute shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

---

## SECTION 9 — WITHDRAWAL AND TERMINATION

**9.1 Voluntary Withdrawal.** The Member may withdraw from the Company by providing ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the Board, in accordance with Section 5.8 of the Operating Agreement.

**9.2 No Withdrawal During Active Projects.** The Member shall not withdraw during an Active Project. If the Company has an Active Project on the date the withdrawal notice would otherwise become effective, the effective date of withdrawal shall be automatically extended to thirty (30) days after the closing of the sale of the Active Project, per Section 4.7(b) of the Operating Agreement.

**9.3 Capital Account Return.** Upon effective withdrawal, the Member's Capital Account balance shall be paid in accordance with Section 4.7 of the Operating Agreement (within sixty (60) days, or in installments over twelve (12) months if the Board determines a lump-sum payment would create undue hardship for the Company).

**9.4 Involuntary Termination / Expulsion.** The Member may be expelled from the Company only for Cause and by Supermajority Vote, subject to the due process requirements of Section 5.9 of the Operating Agreement. "Cause" includes the grounds enumerated in Section 5.9(b) of the Operating Agreement, including material breach, CCB license loss, insurance lapse, felony conviction, willful misconduct, fraud, and safety violations.

**9.5 Effect of Expulsion.** If expelled, the Member's Capital Account shall be paid per Section 4.7(d) of the Operating Agreement (installment terms). The expelled Member forfeits any Labor Share allocation for any Active Project that has not yet reached final sale closing.
