## Amendment #1 — Trade Rate Multipliers

**Sections amended:** Section 8.3(b), Exhibit B

**Changes:**
- Licensed Plumber: 1.2x changed to 1.3x
- Licensed Electrician: 1.2x changed to 1.3x
- Licensed HVAC Technician: 1.2x changed to 1.3x
- Roofing: 1.1x changed to 1.2x
- Carpenter / Framing: 1.0x changed to 1.2x
- Operations / Admin: added as new trade classification at 1.0x
- Project Manager / GC: unchanged at 1.15x
- Painting / Finishing / Drywall: unchanged at 1.0x
- General Labor: unchanged at 1.0x

**Rationale:** Align OA with Ledger's financial models. Licensed trades command a premium in Portland; carpentry was undervalued at 1.0x. Operations/Admin added for completeness.

## Amendment #2 — 30% Completion Gate for Labor Advances

**Section amended:** Section 8.4 (new subsection (b-1) inserted)

**Change:** Added subsection (b-1):

> "No Labor Advance shall be made until the Project has reached at least thirty percent (30%) completion of the approved rehabilitation scope of work, as certified by the Project Manager or Responsible Managing Individual."

**Rationale:** Risk management. Prevents cash outflow before meaningful work is verified. Consistent with M1 decision, Ledger's sign-off conditions, profit-split-model, and advance-tracker tool.

## Amendment #3 — Involuntary Redemption Timeline

**Section reviewed:** Section 11.3

**Change:** None. Section 11.3 already provides for payment within 120 days or in installments over 12 months at the Board's election. This is the correct and intended term.

**Action required elsewhere:** Ledger to update capital-structure.md from 180 days to match OA (120 days + 12-month installment option).

---

## Amendment #4 — Non-Compete Duration

**Section amended:** Section 15.6

**Change:** Non-compete period changed from six (6) months to twelve (12) months following voluntary withdrawal or expulsion.

**Rationale:** Align with Member Agreement. 12 months is standard in Portland construction; provision remains narrowly tailored (house flipping only, Portland metro only) to maximize enforceability.

---

## Amendment #5 — Dual-Signature Threshold

**Section amended:** Section 9.4

**Change:** Dual-signature threshold for bank withdrawals changed from Five Thousand Dollars ($5,000) to Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($2,500).

**Rationale:** Tighter financial controls appropriate for a startup co-op. Align with Ledger's capital-structure.md.

---

## Amendment #6 — Clawback Timeline

**Sections reviewed:** Section 5.8(d), Section 8.4(d)

**Change:** None. Both sections already specify sixty (60) days for repayment of excess Labor Advances. This is the correct and intended term.

**Action required elsewhere:** Ledger to update profit-split-model.md from 90 days to match OA (60 days).
