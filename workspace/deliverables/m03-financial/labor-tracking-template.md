## 1. Purpose

Every dollar in the labor share pool (40% of gross profit) is distributed based on
tracked, verified labor hours. **If you don't log it, you don't get paid for it.**
This template is the standard format for all labor tracking across co-op projects.

---

## 2. Daily Labor Log

Each member fills this out daily. No exceptions.

### Format

| Date | Member | Project | Trade Type | Task Description | Hours | Multiplier | Weighted Hours |
|------|--------|---------|-----------|-----------------|-------|-----------|---------------|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Name | Property Address | Trade code | What you did | Decimal hrs | Auto | Auto |

### Trade Codes & Multipliers

| Code | Trade | Multiplier |
|------|-------|-----------|
| `GEN` | General Labor | 1.00x |
| `PNT` | Painting / Finish Work | 1.00x |
| `FRM` | Framing | 1.20x |
| `CRP` | Carpentry | 1.20x |
| `ROF` | Roofing | 1.20x |
| `PLB` | Plumbing | 1.30x |
| `ELC` | Electrical | 1.30x |
| `HVC` | HVAC | 1.30x |
| `PM` | Project Management | 1.15x |
| `OPS` | Operations / Admin | 1.00x |

### Rules

1. **Log same day.** Don't batch a week of entries on Friday.
2. **Round to nearest 0.25 hour.** (15-minute increments.)
3. **One row per trade type per day.** If you do 4 hours of plumbing and 2 hours of general labor, that's two rows.
4. **Task description is required.** "Worked on the house" is not acceptable. "Installed drain lines in master bath" is.
5. **Minimum loggable block: 1 hour.** No logging 15 minutes of "checking on the site."
6. **Travel time: not logged.** Getting to the job site is on you.
7. **Verified weekly.** Harlan (PM) reviews and signs off on all hours weekly.

## 7. Dispute Resolution

If a member disputes their logged hours:

1. Member raises concern to PM (Harlan) within 7 days of the disputed period
2. PM reviews daily logs, compares to project progress photos and material receipts
3. If unresolved, board reviews at next meeting (majority vote)
4. Board decision is final
5. Adjusted hours are logged with `ADJUSTED` note and reason

---

## 8. Implementation

For the first flip, this tracking will be done via shared spreadsheet (Google Sheets
or equivalent). The template above defines the schema.

For subsequent flips, we should evaluate:
- Dedicated time-tracking software (Clockify, Toggl — both have free tiers)
- Custom tool integrated with `equity_tracker.py`
- Mobile-friendly daily log entry

The key requirement is: **simple enough that a roofer will actually use it at the
end of a long day on the job site.** If it takes more than 2 minutes to log a day's
work, compliance will suffer and the profit split data will be garbage.
